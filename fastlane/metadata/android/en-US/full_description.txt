Liseur is a free, open-source ebook reader for Android. It reads EPUB books from a folder on your phone, and it connects to your own calibre-web, Komga or liseur-sync server so your whole library lives on one shelf. No ads, no trackers, no analytics, no Liseur account. Network access is limited to the book servers and dictionary sources you choose, plus Project Gutenberg when you add free books.

YOUR LIBRARY, ONE SHELF

* Folders on your phone or SD card, indexed with covers
* calibre-web, Komga, liseur-sync and OPDS catalogs merged into the same shelf, with a cloud badge on server books
* Series grouped in reading order, with progress and missing volumes at a glance
* Free books from Project Gutenberg to start you off, before you connect anything

READ YOUR WAY

* Pages or continuous scrolling, per book or library-wide
* Bundled open typefaces (Literata, Vollkorn, Atkinson Hyperlegible, Inter) or the publisher's own
* Margins, line spacing, brightness and four reading themes: light, sepia, dark, and black for OLED screens
* Auto-scroll at your pace, carrying on across chapters
* A footer with the current page, what is left in the chapter, and time remaining
* A refresh mode tuned for Onyx e-ink displays

YOUR PLACE FOLLOWS YOU

* Your reading position syncs across devices through calibre-web, Komga or liseur-sync, down to the exact sentence
* Pair any OPDS catalog with any kosync server as a single Custom connection
* Settings sync and reading statistics across devices when you connect liseur-sync
* Close the book on your phone and pick it up on your tablet

HIGHLIGHTS, NOTES, DICTIONARY

* Highlights in four colours, notes and bookmarks, gathered in a per-book notebook you can export as Markdown
* Search the whole book and jump straight to the passage
* Footnotes open as a card over the page instead of sending you to the back
* Definitions from Wiktionary or an offline dictionary app; online lookups stay off until you turn them on, and you pick the source

FREE SOFTWARE, BY DESIGN

Liseur is built on the open Readium toolkit, with no proprietary dependencies and no Google Play services. Source code, issue tracker and documentation: https://github.com/chmouel/liseur
