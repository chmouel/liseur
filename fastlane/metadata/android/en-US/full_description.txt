Liseur is an open-source EPUB reader and calibre-web client for Android: a modern reader that is equally at home with the files on your phone and with a calibre-web server.

Reading

* Paginated EPUB rendering through the Readium toolkit, full screen by default
* Tap right to go forward, left to go back, the middle for the controls; volume keys work too
* A whole page always fits the screen, with no stray scrolling to catch the last line
* Four reading themes (Light, Sepia, Dark, Black), independent of the app's own theme
* Four bundled open fonts or the publisher's own, plus size, line spacing, margins and brightness
* Time left in the chapter, time left in the book, page or percentage, and a scrubber with chapter ticks

Marking up and looking up

* Highlights in five colours, notes, and bookmarks with a corner ribbon
* A notebook of everything you marked in a book, exportable as Markdown
* Search the whole book, with snippets and jump-to highlighting
* Wiktionary definitions for any selected word, or a hand-off to an offline dictionary app

Your library

* Index folders of EPUBs, covers and all
* Books you are using sort to the front; finished ones get a tick
* Pull to refresh picks up whatever changed behind the app's back

calibre-web

* One screen sets it up: URL, username, password; Liseur works out the catalog, download rights and sync token
* Your server's books merge into the same library with a cloud badge; tap one to download and open it
* Reading positions sync both ways through calibre-web's Kobo protocol
* Remove the copy on the device, or delete the book from the server

Free software

No trackers, no analytics, no proprietary dependencies. The only network traffic is to the calibre-web server you configured and, if you ask for a definition, to Wiktionary.
