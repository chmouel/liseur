You can now import custom TTF and OTF fonts to use while reading. Reading settings allow swapping which side of the screen turns the page forward for easier one-handed reading. You can connect to custom OPDS catalogs and sync reading progress with KOReader sync servers. Reading stats show when you started a book and highlight your reading over the last week. Downloading multiple books at once is more reliable, and page jumps no longer pull you back to where you were.
