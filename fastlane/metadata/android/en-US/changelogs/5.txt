Book covers now show as soon as a Komga or calibre-web library is added, rather than only after a book has been downloaded. An empty library offers to connect a book server. The strip at the foot of the page opens on where you are in the book, and tapping it no longer makes it vanish with no way back. Time left is now worked out from how fast you actually read, learned across every book, so a book you have just opened already knows roughly how long it will take.
