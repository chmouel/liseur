Read books aloud using your phone’s built-in voices or an external speech server, with independent voice settings per language, sleep timers, and background playback. This release also introduces launcher shortcuts, a footer lock in settings, and improved jump navigation with the Back gesture.

### Reading

* Read books aloud using device voices (offline, with no text leaving your phone) or any OpenAI-compatible speech server, including self-hosted Kokoro or cloud services like OpenAI, DeepInfra, OpenRouter, Mistral, and Groq (as well as Gemini in Google Play builds). The docked player lets you adjust speed from 0.75× to 2× without changing pitch, set a sleep timer by minutes or to the end of the chapter, and remembers preferred voices for each language. ([#291](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/291), [#295](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/295))

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c34bd156-455d-4049-8f73-1ff8c55230d8" alt="Read aloud controls docked above the page footer" width="320">

* Read-aloud playback continues in the background while turning pages or navigating elsewhere in the book, volume keys control audio level during playback, and reading can start directly from selected text. ([#295](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/295))
* Choose to send up to five sentences per request to speech servers to reduce pauses between sentences on slower connections. ([#295](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/295))
* A visual voice indicator shows when the reader is waiting for speech audio to load from a server, appearing inside the play button when controls are open and as a status line at the bottom of the page when controls are hidden. ([#300](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/300))

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3644b0a7-27d4-463f-9b98-c7f31208b04a" alt="Preparing the voice label at the bottom of the page" width="320">

* Lock footer changes in settings to prevent accidentally altering or tapping the footer items while reading. ([#299](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/299))

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0d5a7306-f67e-400e-980b-35ca60b881d9" alt="Lock footer toggle in settings" width="320">

* The system Back gesture returns to the page you jumped from whenever the "Back to page N" pill is showing, rather than immediately closing the book. ([#287](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/287))

### Library

* Launcher app shortcuts (accessible by long-pressing the app icon) allow you to jump straight into your most recently read unfinished book, open the library, or view reading statistics. ([#286](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/286))
