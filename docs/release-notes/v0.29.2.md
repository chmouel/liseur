- Reading chips, filters, series and sort controls now fit neatly in the Shelves panel, and you can scroll the chip row when needed.

### Fixes

- The app now opens the library more reliably at startup.
- The related-project links have been updated, including the link to liseur-sync. ([#452](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/452))

### Under the hood

- On Google Play, usage reports now include approximate city and country.
PostHog derives these from the connection and discards the IP address and any
more precise location before saving the report. Only enabled on Google Play,
not on F-Droid or Github releases.
