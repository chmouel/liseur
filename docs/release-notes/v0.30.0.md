This release brings a daily quote from your reading to Home, makes backups more complete, and adds a five-tab floating navigation bar.

### Reading

- Home can show a daily quote from a book you’ve read: your highlight when available, or a passage from up to the furthest point you’ve read. Choose another quote for the day, open a passage without moving your saved place, or turn the card off in Home layout options. Quotes are found on your device. ([#460](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/460))
- Quotes now stay within what you’ve read, and won’t come from hidden books or an old version of a replaced book. Marking a book unread resets its quote progress. ([#460](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/460))
- Home has a refreshed continue-reading card and shelf tiles, with clearer progress and counts.

### Library

- Backups can now include selected offline books and reading sessions. Restore brings back missing books along with reading time, positions, highlights and notes, without adding duplicates. Restored reading time stays on the device and isn’t uploaded to a sync server. ([#459](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/459))
- The floating navigation bar now has Home, Library, Series, Stats and Settings tabs. Stats and Settings are one tap away; switching to Series no longer changes your saved shelf grouping, and navigation keeps its place when you return to a tab. ([#461](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/461))

### Fixes

- Finished-book dates now reflect when a book was actually finished, rather than when it synced. Stats also clarifies that its finished-book count is for books read during the selected period that are now finished.
