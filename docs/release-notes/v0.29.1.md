This release gives Home and Library distinct jobs: Home is a reading desk, while Library is the full catalogue. It also adds Czech and Brazilian Portuguese and makes reading-position sync more reliable when books are marked unread.

### Library

- Home now shows your current book, reading progress, shelves, started series and books to read next. Library has quick filters, series grouping, progress on covers and a compact grid option. Choose which screen opens first in the Library view settings. ([#450](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/450))

### Reading

- Choose Čeština or Português (Brasil) in the app language settings, or let your phone’s language setting choose for you. ([#442](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/442), [#446](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/446))

### Fixes

- Marking a book unread now keeps its reset protected until each sync service has received it. Kobo and Komga resets no longer clear updates still owed to other sync services, and Komga retries pages it could not place. ([#448](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/448))
- Home’s “minutes today” now counts reading sessions on the day they end, matching Statistics. The Series tile includes finished books and switches shelves to series grouping when needed.
- A book marked unread no longer shows its old progress in “Pick up next.”

### Under the hood

- In the Google Play build, anonymous usage and crash reports are on by default. Reports include app interactions or the crash type and code location, but not the error message, book content, titles, notes, server addresses or account details. Turn sharing off in Settings > Advanced; unsent reports are deleted. F-Droid and GitHub builds contain none of this reporting code. ([#449](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/449))
