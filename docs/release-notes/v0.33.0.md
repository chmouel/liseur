This release makes it easier to start reading, reach controls while a book is open, and find your way around your library. It also adds vocabulary sync, Goodreads CSV export, and more ways to work with Hardcover.

### Reading

- Reader controls now open in a bottom sheet that’s easier to reach one-handed. It includes scroll and auto-scroll toggles, reading tools, a visible whole-book translation action, and a book info sheet you can open without leaving the page. ([#539](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/539))
- Word lookup gives offline and online dictionaries equal footing when none is set up, with a shortcut to settings for adding other lookup sites. The selected word and vocabulary controls are easier to spot, and labels wrap on narrow screens or at large text sizes. ([#540](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/540))
- Footnote markers now follow your reading theme, and tapping one opens its note over the page. Dismissing the note—including with system Back—returns you to the same place in the book. ([#527](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/527))
- Saved reading positions restore more reliably in BookOrbit, including in EPUB 2 books and around empty anchors or illustrations. If this device has no verified position, you can choose to resume from the server instead. ([#525](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/525))
- Vocabulary Builder words, definitions, notes, context passages, and review progress now sync across devices through liseur-sync. Your vocabulary stays scoped to its account, and your local words remain on your device when signed out. ([#524](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/524))

### Fixes

- Reading progress now syncs correctly with alternative KOSync servers when no server position exists yet, and standalone KOSync setup handles account creation and sideloaded books more reliably. Komga API keys are no longer forwarded across HTTP redirects. ([#523](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/523))
- A mistyped BookOrbit server address now reports a wrong-server message instead of crashing during sign-in. ([#535](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/535))
