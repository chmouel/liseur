This release makes it easier to see what a book contains and manage its place in your library, while adding more control over page gestures and brightness. Read-aloud sessions now survive closing the app, and OPDS catalog refreshes are more reliable.

### Reading

- Adjust **Page-turn area** from the new **Page taps** screen: choose narrow edges or the familiar wide area, and preview which parts of the page turn, open the menu, or do nothing. Choose Both, Left only, Right only, or Off; with one side, its upper half goes back and lower half goes forward. ([#406](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/406))

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/70017408-7515-4f3f-a2c2-3d166de5098e" alt="Page-tap layouts for Both, Left only, Right only, and Off" width="320">

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/59ed6f55-6246-4b6d-adc8-3f2377657dcc" alt="Left-only page taps with the wide page-turn area" width="320">
- Change screen brightness with an up-or-down swipe along a page edge. Choose the left or right edge, or turn the gesture off; existing taps and page turns still work as before. ([#404](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/404))
- Reopen the app to find paused read-aloud ready to resume at the last sentence. Narration also skips footnote reference marks while leaving the notes themselves readable. ([#401](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/401))

### Library

- Open **Book details** from a book’s actions to see its description, subjects, publication facts, contributors, accessibility information, and reading progress—gathered from the file and connected catalogs. File details such as format, size, and hashes are tucked into a collapsed section. ([#402](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/402))

- Mark a started or finished book unread to put it back on the Unread shelf and open it from the beginning. Its unread status now appears consistently in the library, series, and book details. ([#405](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/405))
- Book actions are grouped by purpose. Long-press a book for its reading and book options; removal and deletion choices are gathered on a separate Manage book page.

### calibre-web

- Catalog entries now keep all listed authors, in the order given, instead of showing only the first. ([#399](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/399))
- OPDS catalogs are more resilient: feeds using other character sets display names correctly, a slow or broken shelf won’t make books on other shelves disappear, and download redirects are checked before the download proceeds. ([#400](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/400))

### Fixes

- If Android can’t open a book’s `content://` stream, Liseur now reports a read error instead of crashing the reader.
