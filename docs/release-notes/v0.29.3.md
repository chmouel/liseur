This release makes reading more reliable when opening a book, returning after a rotation, or closing the reader. Page text stays clear of the navigation bar, and read aloud works better with long books and quick book switches.

### Reading

- The reader waits for a page to be ready after a background rotation or screen resize, and handles pages that disappear during reading features without crashing. ([#456](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/456))
- Reopening a book or closing it while the reader is restoring your saved place no longer risks a crash. ([#454](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/454))
- Page text stays clear of the navigation bar, including when the page is rebuilt in the background.
- Read aloud uses less memory on books with many chapters, and keeps the right cover in its notification when you switch books. ([#454](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/454))
