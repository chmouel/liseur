This release gives reading stats a four-week view, adds offline dictionary lookups, and makes it easier to choose how Liseur’s library is laid out. Reading choices, finished status, ratings and reviews can also follow your books across devices through BookOrbit.

### Reading
- See recent reading at a glance, track a weekly goal, and tap a day in the four-week activity grid to see the books and time read. The stats screens also show book progress, reading pace and details for individual books. ([#480](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/480), [#482](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/482), [#483](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/483), [#491](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/491))

- Install or import StarDict dictionaries for offline lookups. If a word is missed, Liseur can try cleaned-up forms and suggest alternatives. You can also select a word inside a definition to look it up, translate it, save a note, search, copy or share it. ([#485](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/485), [#490](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/490))
- Find selection-toolbar settings under Reading & navigation, preview the available actions, and choose highlight colours from Reading appearance. ([#493](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/493))

### Fixes
- Accented titles and authors sort more naturally. Opening an EPUB with no readable chapters now shows an error instead of crashing, and the last-opened book is restored on a cold start. ([#477](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/477))
