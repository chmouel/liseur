This release adds warmer low-light reading, a full-screen note editor and more options for Companion. It also opens up more free catalogs and improves contributor details and reading-position sync with calibre-web.

### Reading

- Choose Amber, Night red or Charcoal from previews in the theme row. Night red keeps reader labels readable; warm themes fall back to Black on e-ink screens. ([#433](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/433))

- Write notes in a full-screen editor that follows the reading theme, with bold, italics, lists and quotes. Change a highlight’s colour while writing, and keep notes as Markdown for sync, backups and exports. ([#431](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/431))

- Ask Companion where you left off, look up places, objects, groups and ideas as well as characters, and copy or save answers as notes. ([#425](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/425))

- Set Companion answers to Brief, Normal or Detailed. Each length keeps its own saved answers, so you can switch back without asking the AI service again. ([#430](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/430))

- Companion notes now keep headings and lists, with names highlighted for easier reference. Long notes opened from a highlight can be expanded to fill the screen. ([#432](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/432))

### Library

- Browse free catalogs from the chooser, including Project Gutenberg, textos.info, ManyBooks and Gallica. Catalogs are contacted only when you choose one. ([#424](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/424))

- Choose which format to download when a catalog offers more than one, and Liseur remembers your choice for that catalog. ([#423](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/423))

### calibre-web-advanced

- Reading positions sent to calibre-web include their recorded time, and contributor names and roles now follow the book’s details, including for books already in your library. ([#422](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/422))

### Fixes

- Sheets stay in place when flicked at the end of a list or dragged by the handle or title; pulling down still closes them. ([#428](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/428), [#429](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/429))

- Note formatting handles mixed bold and italic selections more cleanly, and line breaks in Companion answers no longer create unintended formatting in saved notes. ([#431](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/431), [#432](https://github.com/chmouel/liseur/pull/432))
